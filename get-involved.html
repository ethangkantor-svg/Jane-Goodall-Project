What to expect

What a Cleanup Day Looks Like

New to this? Here's the whole thing, start to finish.

Check in

Show up at the meeting point, sign in, and grab a bag and gloves. We'll do a quick headcount so nobody gets left behind on the trail.

Safety briefing

A quick rundown on what to pick up (and what not to touch), sun and water safety, and how to flag down a team lead if you need anything.

Clean your section

We split into small groups and cover different stretches of the pathway so we're not tripping over each other, and so we actually cover more ground.

Leave a sign, talk to people

We put up a small sign at the site reminding people to help keep it clean. If there are families or kids out on the pathway, we usually stop to say what we're doing and why. Getting younger kids thinking about this stuff early is honestly half the point.

Sort and weigh

Back at the meeting point, we sort what we collected into recycling, trash, and anything unusual worth noting, then weigh it.

Log the data

Bags, pounds, and volunteer count all get written down. This is the data that shows up on our Impact page.

Wrap up

We say thanks, answer questions, and let you know about the next one. Most cleanups run about two hours, start to finish.

What to bring

Closed-toe shoes, weather-appropriate clothes, a reusable water bottle, and sunscreen. We provide bags, gloves, and grabber tools, so you don't need to own any special gear to show up.
