Support Us

This project runs on a small grant, borrowed supplies, and a lot of volunteer hours. Here's exactly where the money goes, and how you can help.

Where our funding comes from

A ~$250 Roots & Shoots grant, spent carefully.

The Westchester Youth Waterway Initiative is funded through a Roots & Shoots grant of about $250. It's modest, and every dollar is accounted for. Roots & Shoots, the youth program founded by Dr. Jane Goodall and the Jane Goodall Institute, gave us the seed money to get started. Everything since then has been students stretching that grant as far as it goes.

Here's what it covers:

Cleanup supplies

Trash bags, gloves, grabber tools, and first-aid basics for every volunteer at every event.

Educational materials

Printing costs for flyers and the guides on our Education page, plus materials for outreach at local events.

Water-testing kits

Test strips and reagents for the pH, temperature, turbidity, and dissolved oxygen readings we take at the river.

Event organization

Sign-in sheets, site permissions, and the small logistics costs that come with getting a group safely out on the pathway.

Beyond the grant

Sponsor a cleanup, or just help us stretch what we have.

A $250 grant covers the basics, but it doesn't stretch infinitely. If you're a local business, family, or community group interested in supporting a cleanup or covering supplies for an event, we'd love to talk. Sponsorship can be as simple as covering the cost of one cleanup's bags and gloves, or as involved as backing a full season.

Money isn't the only thing that helps. We're just as glad to hear from people who can offer:

Donated supplies Printing / copies Meeting or storage space Water-testing equipment
